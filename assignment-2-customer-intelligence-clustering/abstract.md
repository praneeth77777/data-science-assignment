# Abstract

SegmentForge applies CRISP-DM to customer segmentation using the UCI Online Retail dataset. The raw audit covered 541,909 transaction lines and documented 135,080 missing customer identifiers, 5,268 exact duplicate rows, 9,288 cancellation-invoice rows, and nonpositive quantity or price records before preparation. Completed positive-value purchases produced Recency, Frequency, and Monetary features for 4,338 customers. Customer identifiers were used only for grouping and a deterministic 79.4%/20.6% discovery-audit partition.

A bounded hill-climbing experiment manager evaluated 42 configurations across K-Means, Gaussian Mixture, and Agglomerative clustering; feature views; transformations; scaling; outlier policies; and cluster counts. Thirty-four trials satisfied cluster-size constraints and eight were rejected. The locked winner used K-Means with four clusters, RFM features, a Yeo–Johnson transformation, StandardScaler, and retained extreme customers. Discovery silhouette was 0.3331, locked-audit silhouette was 0.3383, and discovery subsample stability was adjusted Rand index 0.9888 ± 0.0025. The resulting personas describe high-value frequent, established mid-value, recent occasional, and long-recency customers. An eight-page Streamlit administration dashboard exposes data quality, cluster profiles, masked customer lookup, the experiment leaderboard, explanations, temporal monitoring context, and deployment metadata. The project treats segments as descriptive hypotheses rather than causal effects, lifetime-value predictions, or guaranteed campaign outcomes.
